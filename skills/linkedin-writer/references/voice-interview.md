# Voice interview (use when the person has no writing samples)

Ask these one at a time, in this order. Capture the answers in their own words, quoting them. Then write VOICE.md.

1. **Who are you writing to?** Describe one real person who should read your posts (job, problem, what keeps them up).
2. **What do you know better than most people around you?** Three topics, narrow ones. ("Fixing slow Power BI reports in finance teams", not "data".)
3. **Tell me about a mistake you made at work that taught you something.** Tell it the way you would tell a friend over coffee. (Record this verbatim; it shows the real rhythm and vocabulary.)
4. **Which three posts or authors on LinkedIn do you actually enjoy reading, and why?** (Why matters more than who.)
5. **Which LinkedIn habits make you cringe?** (These become the "never" list.)
6. **How do you talk at work?** Formal, warm, blunt, dry, playful? Do you swear, joke, use short sentences? Any phrases you repeat?
7. **What are you comfortable sharing publicly, and what is off limits?** (Clients, numbers, personal life, employer.)
8. **If a reader remembers one thing about you after a month, what should it be?**

Then reflect back a 5-line summary ("You sound like..., you write about..., you never...") and ask: "Is that you?" Correct it before saving.

## VOICE.md template
```
# Voice profile: <name>
Sounds like: <2-3 specific lines, e.g. "calm and direct; explains like a senior colleague at a whiteboard">
Sentences: <typical length, fragments allowed?>
Uses: <words/phrases they use>
Never: <words/habits that would sound wrong>
Opens with: <patterns>
Closes with: <patterns>
Formatting: <paragraph length, lists, emoji, caps>
Owns these topics: <3 narrow topics>
Reader: <the one person they write to>
Off limits: <what not to share>
Sample lines: <3 lines in their voice>
Corrections log: <date - what they changed and why>
```

# Self-edit checklist (run before showing a draft)

## Truth
- [ ] Every number, name, date and claim came from the author. Nothing invented.
- [ ] Anything about a client or employer is allowed to be public (ask if unsure).
- [ ] No promise of results the author cannot back up.

## First line
- [ ] Specific, under ~140 characters, makes the right reader want line two.
- [ ] Not a greeting, announcement or scene-setting.

## Body
- [ ] One idea. If you can summarise it in a sentence, that sentence is in the post.
- [ ] A concrete detail in every paragraph (number, tool, moment).
- [ ] Short paragraphs; rhythm varies; no wall of one-line paragraphs either.
- [ ] Read aloud: nothing the author would not say.
- [ ] Cut 10-20%. The best sentence is often the one after the one you wanted to keep.

## AI tells (delete or rewrite)
- Opening or closing with a summary of what you just said ("In conclusion", "Ultimately")
- "delve", "leverage", "unlock", "game-changer", "landscape", "tapestry", "navigate", "robust", "seamless", "elevate", "synergy"
- "It's not just X, it's Y" and other tidy contrast formulas
- Three-item lists where two items are padding
- Em dashes in every other sentence
- Hedging stacks ("it's important to note that perhaps")
- A moral at the end that repeats the hook
- Perfectly balanced sentences all the same length

## Close
- [ ] Ends on the point or one honest question. No bait ("comment YES", "tag someone", "repost").
- [ ] At most one call to action.
- [ ] 3-5 specific hashtags on the last line. Links in the first comment.

Then run `li-lint --file post.txt` and fix errors; treat warnings as questions.

# Hook patterns (the first line)

A hook promises something specific enough that the right reader needs the second line. Write five and pick one. Examples use made-up details; replace with the author's real ones.

| Pattern | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Number + result** | A specific outcome with a number | "Our month-end report took 9 hours. Now it takes 40 minutes." |
| **Mistake confession** | You were wrong, and here is the cost | "I rebuilt the same dashboard four times before I asked one question." |
| **Contrarian, but earned** | A belief that goes against the habit, with grounds | "Most dashboards fail before the first chart is drawn." |
| **Specific scene** | One concrete moment, no setup | "At 11:40 pm the CFO asked why two reports showed different revenue." |
| **Plain instruction** | Teaches one thing, tells the reader what they get | "How to find which Power BI visual is slowing your report (2 minutes)." |
| **Before / after** | Contrast in one line | "Same data. One report nobody opened, one report the board reads first." |
| **The question the reader has** | The exact thing they type into search | "Why does my DAX measure return blank?" |
| **Rule you now follow** | A personal rule and its origin | "I stopped saying yes to 'quick' report requests. Here is why." |
| **Overheard line** | A real sentence a client or colleague said | "'Can we just see it in Excel?' - the most honest feedback I got this year." |
| **Counterintuitive stat** | A true number that surprises, with source | "9 of the 12 reports I audited had never been opened." |

## Testing a hook
- Could a stranger tell what the post is about? If not, make it more specific.
- Does it need the next line to make sense? That is fine. Does it give nothing away? Rewrite.
- Is it under about 140 characters? The fold is near.
- Would you say it out loud? If not, change the words.

## Avoid
"I'm excited to announce", "Let me tell you a story", "Unpopular opinion:", "Here's what nobody tells you", "Stop scrolling", questions that have an obvious yes, and any line that could open a thousand other posts.

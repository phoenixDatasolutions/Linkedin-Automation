# Nine post formats (pick one per post)

Each has a skeleton. Fill with the author's real detail. Length guide is for the body, in characters.

## 1. Lesson from a mistake (700-1,200)
Hook: the mistake, in one line -> what happened (2-3 short lines, with a detail) -> what it cost -> what you do now (the rule) -> one line for the reader's version of the problem.

## 2. Case study / result (800-1,300)
Hook: the result with a number -> the situation before (1-2 lines) -> the 2-3 changes that mattered (short list) -> what did NOT matter (honesty builds trust) -> who this fits.
(Never name a client without permission; "a finance team of 40" is enough.)

## 3. How-to in 5 steps (900-1,500)
Hook: the outcome and the time it takes -> numbered steps, one line each, each with the "why" in a few words -> the common trap -> where to look next (link in first comment).

## 4. Contrarian take (600-1,000)
Hook: the belief you reject -> why people believe it (be fair) -> what you saw that contradicts it -> what you do instead -> when the common advice IS right.

## 5. Behind the scenes / process (700-1,200)
Hook: something you are building or fixing right now -> the problem in plain words -> what you tried -> what is working/not -> what you will do next. Works well as a series.

## 6. Story with a point (900-1,500)
Hook: the moment -> the tension -> the turn -> the point in one line. Keep the scene concrete (time, place, what was said). Cut anything that does not serve the point.

## 7. Checklist / list (500-900)
Hook: "N things I check before X" -> list of 5-8 items, each 1 line with a reason -> the one item most people skip. Lists get saved; keep them specific.

## 8. Poll (question under 140 characters, 2-4 options under 30 characters each)
Post text: why you are asking (2-3 lines, your own view withheld) -> poll. Follow up later with the result and what you learned. Only ask what you genuinely want to know.

## 9. Document / carousel outline (PDF, 6-12 slides)
Slide 1: the promise. Slides 2-n: one idea per slide, big text, 20-30 words max. Last slide: the takeaway and where to follow. Post text: 2-3 lines that explain what the reader gets. (Publish with `li-post document ... --title`.)

## Choosing
| If you have... | Use |
|---|---|
| a number and a before/after | 2 |
| a failure | 1 |
| a skill others lack | 3 or 7 |
| a disagreement with the usual advice | 4 |
| something in progress | 5 |
| a vivid moment | 6 |
| a real question | 8 |
| material that needs structure | 9 |

## Cadence
Two to four posts a week beats daily mediocre ones. Mix: about half teaching (3, 7, 9), a quarter stories (1, 6), a quarter opinion/process (4, 5). Re-use a strong idea in a different format after a few weeks.
